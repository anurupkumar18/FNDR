  • Five-user task study, planned30 minutes each with a fixed Resume task, a System Usability Scale score, and quotes. Felipe leads with Anurup.
  • Success target4 of 5 users finish the Resume task unaided.

Our thoughts

The one number we care about is time to correctly recover prior work, compared with the person's normal tools. The conversations come first so the task script reflects how people really resume work, not how we assume they do.

Questions for the course staff draft, team to confirm

  • Do sessions with classmates or outside volunteers need consent or review, since FNDR records on-screen content?
  • Should participants use a seeded sample profile instead of their own Mac, to avoid collecting real data?
  • Can the course help recruit five people outside the team?
  • Is a SUS score plus task time enough, or do you want a different measure?
  • Our plan runs the full study before the Final (Dec 14), with discovery conversations before Beta. Is that the right timing?
